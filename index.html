Biography

I am an Assistant Professor at the School of Economics and Management, China University of Petroleum (Beijing). I received my PhD from the School of Economics and Management at Beihang University in 2023, where I was advised by Professor Ying Fan and Associate Professor Chunan Wang, and I spent my doctoral years in a joint training program at North Carolina State University with Professor Xiaoyong Zheng.

My research and teaching focus on empirical industrial organization, applied microeconomics, and applied econometrics, with a particular emphasis on using structural estimation and causal inference to address policy relevant questions in environmental regulation and sustainable transportation. Much of my recent work studies the low carbon transition of air transport, covering emission reduction pathways, sustainable aviation fuel adoption, and competition between airlines and high speed rail.

I also serve as an Adjunct Associate Researcher at the Green and Low Carbon Aviation Center of the Laboratory for Low Carbon Governance and Intelligent Policy (LLIG) at Beihang University.

Research Interests

Substantive areas: empirical industrial organization, aviation decarbonization, carbon markets and carbon pricing, automobile markets and vehicle policy, energy and climate policy.

Methods: structural estimation, causal inference, applied econometrics, and game theory.
